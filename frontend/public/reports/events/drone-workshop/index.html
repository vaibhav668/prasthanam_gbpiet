About Drone Assembly

Drone assembly is the end-to-end engineering process of integrating mechanical structures, aerodynamic rotors, propulsion systems, and onboard flight intelligence to make an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) fully airworthy.

The workshop was conducted across multiple intensive hands-on sessions where participants performed physical frame mounting, motor alignment, electronic speed controller (ESC) soldering, power distribution, and flight controller configuration.

"Before the flight, there's a team that believes...."

Core Drone Subsystems & Assembly Modules

Frame & Structural Chassis High-strength lightweight quadcopter arms and central mounting plates for structural rigidity and vibration dampening.
BLDC Motors & Propellers High-KV brushless DC motors paired with counter-rotating (CW/CCW) propellers engineered for optimal thrust-to-weight ratio.
Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs) Fast-switching MOSFET driver circuits delivering regulated multi-phase current to synchronize motor RPM under flight control commands.
Flight Controller & Battery Onboard micro-controller with gyroscope/accelerometer IMUs and high-discharge LiPo battery power harness.
